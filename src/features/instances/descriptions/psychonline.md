Psych Online is a multiplayer modification for Psych Engine that adds online lobbies and synchronized rhythm gameplay to the familiar *Friday Night Funkin'* formula.

## Highlights

- Online lobbies for playing songs with other people.
- Synchronized note charts and song progress between players.
- In-game chat and lobby management.
- Built on Psych Engine, so the usual Lua and HScript modding workflow is available.
- Support for custom songs and stages in online sessions.

## Notes

- Multiplayer features depend on the mod's online services being available.
- WeekBox only offers release assets published for your operating system.
- Make sure every player uses compatible engine and mod versions before starting a session.
