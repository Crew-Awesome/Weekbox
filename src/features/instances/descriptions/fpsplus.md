FPS Plus is Rozebud's performance-focused *Friday Night Funkin'* engine. It keeps the classic game structure while improving input handling and allowing the game to run at higher frame rates.

## Highlights

- Improved input accuracy for timing-sensitive charts.
- Custom keybinds and configurable controls.
- High and uncapped frame-rate support.
- Quality-of-life options for practice and performance.
- Compatibility with many familiar base-game assets and mods.

## Notes

- WeekBox only offers release assets published for your operating system.
- Mods that depend on Psych-specific scripting may not work without adaptation.
- Very high frame-rate targets depend on your display and hardware.
