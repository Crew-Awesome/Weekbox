Psych Engine is the most established community engine for *Friday Night Funkin'*. Maintained by Shadow Mario, it keeps the familiar base-game structure while exposing a deep scripting layer that lets mods change almost every part of a song without touching the engine source.

## Highlights

- **Lua scripting** for songs, stages, characters, events, and UI.
- **HScript support** for lower-level engine customization.
- Custom weeks, difficulties, note types, note splashes, and events.
- Shader support and per-song camera and visual overrides.
- Built-in chart, character, and week editors.

## Mod structure

Most mods are installed as a folder inside `mods/`. A typical mod contains its own `data`, `images`, `songs`, `scripts`, and `weeks` folders, so multiple mods can coexist without replacing the base game.

## Notes

- WeekBox only offers release assets published for your operating system.
- Keep the engine folder intact: scripts and assets are resolved relative to it.
- Back up your `mods/` folder before moving between major engine versions.
